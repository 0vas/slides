# Olympus onboarding

Audience: technical teams starting with Olympus. Current scope supersedes the earlier workshop: exactly 20 Spanish slides, no introduction/profile slide, no exercises or timers. One continuous onboarding verifies connections, modules and delegation in the actual application.

Acceptance criteria: nine interactive guidance states provide concrete actions, observable evidence and failure handling. Slide controls never claim to authenticate or execute Olympus. Preserve keyboard navigation, reduced motion, official favicon and documentation embedding. Keep participant material consistent with the current deck.

Validation: production build, qa.cjs (all 20 slides, nine onboarding states, bounds, local assets and downloads), motion.cjs (normal/reduced motion), and documentation workshop-embed.cjs (English/Spanish shell, desktop/mobile, iframe navigation, fullscreen and favicon).
